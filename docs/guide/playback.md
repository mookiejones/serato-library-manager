# Play a track

Play a file that is already in the library. The app streams it from this computer. It does not upload the file, write to it, or add a Serato history row.

1. Click a track.
2. Double-click the row, or click the play button on the row. In the detail pane, click **Play**.
3. The bar shows the title and the artist, **Pause**, a seek bar, the elapsed time, the total time, and **Volume**.

**Space** pauses and resumes. It does nothing while the cursor is in a text field. Press **Space** with a track selected and nothing playing to start that track.

Seek by dragging the bar. The time on the left is where you are. The time on the right is the length.

MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, and OGG are the types the server will send. If this window cannot play a file, the bar says **This file cannot be played in this window.**

A track that is not in the library is not played. A shortcut to a file outside the library is not followed.
