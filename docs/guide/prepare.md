# Prepare

Prepare is Serato's holding list for tracks you might play next. This app can add and remove tracks there, and it can browse the Prepare space.

## From a track

1. Quit Serato DJ Pro. See [Safety and backups](safety.md).
2. Open the track.
3. Click **Send to Prepare**.

The bar says **Sent to Prepare.** The button then reads **Remove from Prepare**. Click it to take the track back out. The bar says **Removed from Prepare.**

The audio file stays where it is. The track also stays in the crates it was already in.

## The Prepare space

1. Open the space menu.
2. Choose **Prepare**.

You are now looking at Prepare's own crates and tracks. **All tracks** in this space is the Prepare list. **New**, **Rename**, **Color**, and **Delete** work here the same way as in [Crates](crates.md).

Choose **Library** in the space menu to go back to the main library.
